Frequently Asked Questions

How much notice is required for custom cakes? +
We recommend ordering celebration cakes at least 48 hours in advance through our Custom Cake Studio. For multi-tier wedding cakes and large corporate orders, 2 to 3 weeks notice is preferred.
Do you accommodate dietary allergies? +
Yes! We offer certified Gluten-Free almond cakes and 100% plant-based vegan cupcakes. Please note that while we follow strict sanitation protocols, our facility handles wheat, dairy, and tree nuts.
How does local cake delivery work? +
All cakes and delicate pastries are transported in our temperature-controlled chilled vans by trained bakery couriers, ensuring your creation arrives pristine and chilled.
Can I customize the greeting text on my cake? +
Absolutely! When using our Custom Cake Studio, you can type your exact greeting into the Inscription Plaque input and see it appear in real-time on the cake stand.
